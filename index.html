06 / FAQ

The honest answers.

Will my customers know it's automated?
Possibly, yes. Joe is natural and conversational, but she doesn't pretend to be human — and most customers don't care, especially when their text gets answered at 9pm on a Saturday. The alternative is no answer at all.
What if she gets an answer wrong?
Joe sticks to what's in your profile — your services, prices, hours, what you don't do. She won't invent things. If a customer asks something Joe doesn't know, she says she'll check with you and takes their details. Every conversation is logged in your Google Sheet, so you can see exactly what was said.
What about urgent jobs?
Joe is trained to spot urgency — gas leaks, flooding, no power, electrical danger, sewer overflows, anything that can't wait. The moment she flags it, you get an SMS with the customer's number, what they said, and what she told them. On phone calls, Joe also tries to transfer the customer to you live, and only falls back to SMS if you don't pick up.
Do I need a new phone number?
Yes — your service includes a dedicated AU mobile number, set up by us. Use it on your business cards, website, vehicle signage, Google Business listing. If you want to keep your existing number front-of-house, you can forward calls to it. Happy to walk through the options on the setup call.
What happens if I cancel?
Month-to-month, no lock-in. Cancel any time. Your conversation log stays in your Google Sheet — you keep every customer interaction. We deactivate the number and Joe within 24 hours.
Where is this available?
Australia-wide. Workbench is based on the Sunshine Coast and supports tradies in every state and territory. Works for any AU mobile or landline.
